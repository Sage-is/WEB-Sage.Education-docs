**Welcome Sage.is AI-UI Contributors!** 

There are many ways to get involved and help make Sage.is AI-UI even better. As with all Open Source projects, we aren't just code. We are a community. Even letting us when you run into a bug helps us create a better Sage.is AI-UI. 

Here are the four quickest ways to contribute:

- Found something that's not working right? [Let us know!](/contribute/Report%20Issues) Every bug report helps us improve the experience for everyone.
- Do you enjoy writing? [Help create clearer, more helpful documentation Sage.is AI-UI](/contribute/Document%20and%20Make%20Tutorials). Whether it's fixing typos, adding examples, your contributions make the project more accessible.
- Are are you multilingual? [Help translate Sage.is AI-UI](/contribute/Help%20Translate%20Sage.is%20AI-UI), let us empower users around the world independent of what language they speak.
- Are you a master coder or just getting started? [Dive into the code and help with development](/contribute/Help%20Code%20Sage.is%20AI-UI). We welcome pull requests for bug fixes, new features, and performance improvements.

**Getting Started**  
Check out our [Code Contribution Guidelines](/contribute/Sage.is%20AI-UI%20Code%20Contribution%20Guidelines) for detailed information on our processes, coding standards, and how to submit your first contribution.

**Questions?** Join our [community discussions](https://sage.is/community/) to connect with other contributors and the core team.

---

Together, let's create the best Open Source AI tool for humanity. 🎉
